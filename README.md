## GLP-1 storage and handling

Wegovy, Ozempic, Zepbound and Mounjaro all specify refrigerated storage at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) and instruct against freezing.

The number that decides whether a dose is still good is the other one: how long the product may be out of the refrigerator. That figure changes with manufacturer and presentation and is not a property of the molecule.
